# ADR-006 Mock SMS and Teams adapters
**Status: Decided by founder (mock); design Proposed.** `MessagingProvider` and `StaffAlertProvider` have SIM implementations writing to `ops.message` / `ops.teams_activity` with `source_mode=SIMULATED`; UI shows a phone-style inbox per persona and a Teams-style panel. Twilio and Bot Framework adapters are stubs that throw unless `APP_DATA_CLASS != SYNTHETIC` and live gates pass (never in P1). Inbound guardian replies come from the demo panel through the same webhook handler a real Twilio POST would hit (signature check replaced by a sim HMAC). Evidence: PRD §9, §10, §16.
