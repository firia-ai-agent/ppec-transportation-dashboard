# PPEC transportation dashboard: EV-01 to EV-20 verification

By Athena (research lead), 2026-10-14 (ET). Read-only research. Source: `/workspace/ppec/PRD-v1.0.txt`, Appendix A.4 (line 1997). Every URL was checked 2026-10-14.

## Summary (one screen)

**Status counts (20 checks):** Verified 8 · Contradicted 1 · Unverified 11.

**Top risks**
1. **Bedrock is HIPAA-eligible as a service, but model-level coverage is unclear (EV-01).** The AWS list (last updated 2026-09-03) names "Amazon Bedrock" with no model carve-out on the page I fetched. A secondary source (Aptible) says Fable and Mythos models are excluded. Anthropic's own page says Covered Models such as Claude Fable 5 need 30-day retention and can't run with zero data retention (ZDR). Don't route PHI to any specific Claude model on Bedrock until AWS confirms it in writing.
2. **The FCC TCPA citation is wrong (EV-13, Contradicted).** 11 Apr 2025 is when the *2024* revocation rules took effect. FCC 26-67 was adopted 30 Sep 2026 and released 1 Oct 2026. It has **no effective date yet**: it takes effect 30 days after Federal Register publication, which hadn't happened by 13 Oct 2026.
3. **Several messaging cost and coverage facts are still open** (EV-07, EV-08, EV-11, EV-16). Twilio Editions pricing isn't published, D3's $71.91 vetting fee couldn't be traced, and group-MMS receipt behaviour needs an experiment.
4. **Teams guest coverage under the Microsoft BAA isn't confirmed (EV-09).** Microsoft's HIPAA page puts Teams in scope but doesn't mention guests. The PRD default (no guests) stands.

**Blocks real data.** FOR INFORMATION, outside the current lock. None of this blocks the synthetic proof of concept.
- **OpenRouter offers no HIPAA BAA.** OpenRouter says so itself: https://openrouter.ai/blog/insights/governing-team-ai-spend/. The lock already limits it to synthetic data, but no real child or guardian data may reach it.
- **Vercel** signs BAAs (https://vercel.com/kb/guide/hipaa-compliance-guide-vercel). Earlier research found this needs Pro plus an add-on, or Enterprise; I didn't re-check the tier today.
- **Neon** offers HIPAA only on the Scale plan, with a BAA with Databricks (https://neon.com/docs/security/hipaa).
- **Before any real data:** a signed BAA with every vendor that touches PHI, and only HIPAA-eligible services used (AWS via Artifact, Twilio, Anthropic or Bedrock).
- **Google Maps** terms bar HIPAA data and COPPA child-directed apps (EV-15). It stays limited to the sealed benchmark.

**Locks respected.** ACCEPTED by founder via Firia, 2026-10-14:
- the proof of concept runs on Vercel + Neon, moving to AWS later
- SMS, Teams, GPS and vehicle configs are mocked, with demo reset buttons
- the demo area is 2216 Jog Rd, Greenacres FL
- a live OpenRouter agent runs on synthetic data only
- auth is Auth.js
- the FQ-01 to FQ-12 defaults are accepted

Nothing below proposes changing these.

**Regulatory posture.** Children with disabilities attend a PPEC (prescribed pediatric extended care) centre, a healthcare provider, so their transport, attendance and needs data is likely PHI.
- **COPPA:** the tool is adult-facing only (PRD CQ-11). A formal COPPA opinion is still an open client question.
- **FERPA:** not likely to apply to a healthcare provider. UNVERIFIED; counsel to confirm.
- **Florida state law:** not assessed. UNVERIFIED.
