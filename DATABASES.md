## Migrations in repo
**Frozen:** a migration is frozen once it has been applied to any shared Neon branch. 0000 and 0001 were applied to previews and production on 2026-10-14; 0002 and 0003 followed (production build of main @ b1df48c), so 0000–0003 are all frozen. Never edit a frozen file: fix forward with a new migration.

**Before a merge that migrates production, the founder takes a Neon restore point** (a snapshot of `main`, the one database — DECISION-LEDGER #43). That is the rollback for anything that has touched rows; the Rollback column below is the schema-only answer, and it cannot bring back data a migration changed or dropped. The two go together: a prod-migrating merge is the restore point *plus* a written rollback, and the prod-release-gate (`MIGRATE_PROD_ON_BUILD=1` on `main`) is what makes that moment identifiable in advance. Nothing in CI runs a rollback file; they are reference, and none of them has been run.

**Current restore point: `pre-0008-2026-10-15`** — a manual Neon snapshot of `main`, taken by the founder at 9:54 AM ET on 2026-10-15 with 0007 applied, no expiry. Two limits shape how it is used:
- **The Neon Free plan allows exactly one manual snapshot.** There is no history of restore points — taking the next one replaces this one. So the snapshot is retaken (and this name bumped) immediately before each migrating merge, and at any moment the only restorable pre-migration state is the one named here. Record the new name in this section when it is replaced.
- **Neon's restore window is 6 hours.** Point-in-time restore reaches back 6 hours only; past that, the manual snapshot above is the sole way back. A bad migration therefore has a 6-hour window in which either mechanism works, and after that only the snapshot — which is why the snapshot is taken before the merge rather than after a problem appears.

There is no 0004: the thread uniqueness index was renumbered to 0006 to sit behind main's 0005 (commit 1e10495), and the gap is left as it is because a migration file's number is its identity in `drizzle.__drizzle_migrations`.
